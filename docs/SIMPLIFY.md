# Simplifying turbo: what earns its keep

Turbo has three plan tiers plus the code around them: adapt, the plan cache, serving, per-connection state and
QC_STOCK. This report measures what each part does on real applications' statements, then ranks the ways to make it
smaller without losing speed or correctness. The measurements were taken on 2026-11-01 against `main` at `56bb17c`.
The top options are draft PRs:

| PR | what | lines |
|---|---|---|
| this one | this report; the settled A/B switches and the specialised plans' lanes deleted | qc/ −141 |
| the next, stacked on it | the specialised plan tier retired; qcg and qvm take its shapes | qc/ and tests −600 |

Each PR description has its test results and its Xeon A/B.

### Which tier serves what

Every statement in sqlgauntlet's corpus (1,301 statements from 44 applications) was run 40 times, hot and with a
prepare per execution, with `QC_STATS=1` on `main`'s serving build (macOS arm64). The counts are executions per tier:

| | specialised | qcg | qvm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| statements with any execution, hot | 1 | 29 | 972 |
| statements with any execution, prep | 1 | 29 | 972 |
| executions served, hot | 41 | 518 | 36,007 |

- **The specialised tier** served one statement: `chromium.segment.url_for_segment`, a rowid point with a join by
  rowid (6 µs at stock). Its feed and lookup shapes came from the twelve-dollar challenge's API. No application in the
  corpus has the feed's shape.
- **qcg** lifted 29 statements. Adapt kept 4 of them:
  - `dagster.runs.tag_values_typeahead`;
  - `karakeep.lists.stats.manual_sizes`;
  - `karakeep.lists.stats.smart.untagged`;
  - `technitium.querylogs.count.blocked`.

  The other 25 lost their learning phase and were parked. qcg's point lookups are also handed to qvm after their first
  run ("g lookup", `qg_exec`).
- **qvm** serves everything else that is served.

On the 30 statements that touched the specialised tier or qcg, a local A/B (3 rounds, arms rotated, macOS; µs
statements swing ±30 % here, so only large effects count) compared `main` with `QC_VM_ONLY=1`, which lifts straight
into qvm. That is what retiring both non-qvm tiers would do:

| | `main` / stock | `QC_VM_ONLY` / stock | geomean, qvm-only / `main` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| hot | 0.920 | 0.962 | 1.046 |
| prep | 0.965 | 1.051 | 1.089 |

Two statements carry most of that loss, both `count(*)` over an index equality that qcg counts by walking the leaf
cells (`GLevel.cntEq`):

| statement | hot: `main` / stock | hot: `QC_VM_ONLY` / stock |
|---|---:|---:|
| `technitium.querylogs.count.blocked` | 0.19 | 0.69 |
| `memos.count_user_memos` | 0.61 | 0.95 |

So qcg still earns its keep on those counts. The specialised tier doesn't: its one statement goes to qcg when it is
retired.

### Duplication and dead code found

- **Tiers that overlap.** qcg and qvm both run index scans, seeks, rowid points, IN lists, joins, correlated
  subqueries, aggregates, GROUP BY, sorts and top-N. The specialised tier's shapes (index scan with a limit, rowid
  point, joins by rowid, correlated `count(*)`) are a subset of qcg's.
- **Dead or settled variants.** These are deleted in this PR:
  - `QC_LANES` and its 117 lines (off since RESULTS 5e: slower on the Xeon, hot and cold);
  - `QC_GEN_LOOKUPS`, `QV_NUMCOL=int`, `QV_NOHASH`, `QV_NOTOPN`, `QC_NOSTAMP`.

  Each was the losing arm of an A/B that set today's default, and nothing (tests, bindings, sqlgauntlet's arms) sets
  them. These are left for after #5, which edits the same code:
  - `QC_ADAPT_SMALL_US` / `QC_ADAPT_SMALL_WIN`, off since the rule couldn't see the losses it targeted;
  - `QC_PARK`, `QC_NOGATE` / `QC_NOSCANGATE` and `QC_NOREOFFER`, A/B arms whose questions are answered.

  `QV_NOREGION`, `QC_NOCACHE`, `QC_STREAM*`, `QC_NOADAPT`, `QC_VM_ONLY` and `QC_STATS*` stay: the tests and diagnosis
  use them.
- **Specialised-only helpers.** These go with the specialised tier:
  - `QC_ORACLE`;
  - the generated decoders' signature dispatch (`qc_dec_run`, `qc_dec_code`, used by nothing else);
  - `qc_count1` / `qc_counts`, which duplicate qcg's cell-walk count.
- **Per-opcode copy-paste.** Little is left. qvm's opcode bodies are already shared between `qv_run` and the regions
  ("opcode bodies shared by qv_run and the regions"). The region handlers are in-line fast paths that fall back to
  those bodies.
- **QC_STOCK.** 188 lines of side tables and patch.py's `--copies` (generated at build, not committed). It's a shipped
  mode and has no duplicate to remove.
- **Test harness overlap.** Every differential suite runs twice, plans first and `QC_VM_ONLY=1`, and regress builds
  five binaries. While qcg exists both modes are needed; without it the second run of each suite goes.
- **Unused:** `qv_fb_dump` (a debugging helper, never called).

## What was tried and dropped

- **Retiring qcg now.** The `QC_VM_ONLY` A/B above: −4.6 % hot and −8.9 % prep on its statements, from the counts. It
  stays until qvm has a count path (option 3).
- **Retiring both tiers at once** (`QC_VM_ONLY` as the default): dropped for the same reason.

## Method

- **Tier counts.** `QC_STATS=1` per statement (one process each) on sqlgauntlet's driver and built databases, 40
  executions, hot and prep, `main` 56bb17c. The aggregation scripts and per-statement counts are in the shared
  session notes (`turbo-simplify-tiers.tsv`).
- **The local A/B.** sqlgauntlet's driver, arms rotated per round, 3 rounds, `REPS=300`, `BUDGET=300` ms.
- **The Xeon A/Bs.** box1 through the broker, using the corpusfast harness of exp1352. Each run is the PR (arm
  `serve`) against its base (arm `serve_b`) and stock, 4 rounds with arm order rotating, hot and prep, user-mode
  instructions and cycles. They cover two sets:
  - the 30 statements above;
  - 41 hot-small guards, one per project, all served by qvm.

  Experiment ids and results are in each PR's description.
